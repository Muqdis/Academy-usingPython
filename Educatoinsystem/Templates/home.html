About Us

Welcome to our Academy Platform, a modern web-based application built with Django to connect students and tutors in a secure, interactive environment. What makes our platform unique is its dual purpose: we provide free educational support for students who cannot afford tuition and empower tutors who wish to contribute to society, while also offering full academic management features for both students and tutors. Our platform caters to four types of users: Paid Students: Students who enroll in regular courses with professional tutors. Free Students: Students in need of financial support who receive guidance from voluntary tutors. Paid Tutors: Tutors who offer their expertise as a service and earn through their teaching. Volunteer Tutors: Tutors who provide education freely to support students and contribute to societal development.
