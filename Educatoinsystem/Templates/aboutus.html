About Us

Welcome to our Education System platform! Our mission is to provide quality education to every student, regardless of their financial background, and to empower tutors who want to make a positive impact in society.

Our Mission

We believe that education is the key to societal development. Our platform connects students who cannot afford tuition with tutors who either want to teach for free or earn by teaching. Through this system, we aim to:

  • Promote equal access to quality education.
  • Encourage community service and social responsibility among tutors.
  • Support sustainable learning through online and in-person sessions.
  • Empower students to achieve their academic and personal goals.

We are committed to building a society where knowledge is shared freely and education becomes a right, not a privilege.
